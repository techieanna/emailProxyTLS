About Us

Fife Hindu Temple and Cultural Centre is a registered charity dedicated to serving the Hindu community in Fife and surrounding areas. We provide a welcoming space for worship, cultural activities, and community engagement.

Our Purposes

  • Advancement of religion through Hindu worship and spiritual practices
  • Advancement of citizenship and community development
  • Advancement of arts, heritage, culture and science
  • Provision of recreational facilities for the community

Our Vision

To create a vibrant spiritual and cultural hub where people of all backgrounds can come together to learn, celebrate, and grow.
